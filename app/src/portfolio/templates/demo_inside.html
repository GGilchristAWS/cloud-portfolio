How the demo works

  1. LaunchThis page calls a Lambda through CloudFront; at most four launches a day
  2. DeployGitHub Actions runs Terraform as a scoped AWS role, signed in with OIDC: no keys
  3. RunCloudFront and a load balancer in front of two Fargate tasks in two zones (more under load), plus a monitoring task
  4. ObserveOpenTelemetry into Prometheus, Tempo and Loki, shown in Grafana
  5. Tear downEventBridge Scheduler runs the Teardown workflow two hours later

What you'll see

  • The app's home page shows which container and availability zone answered you; reload to watch the load balancer switch.
  • Grafana: golden signals, the SLO's error budget and a latency heatmap. Click a dot on the latency graph to open that request's trace; every log line links to its trace too.
  • Background traffic, labeled synthetic, keeps the graphs moving from the first minutes.
  • The Deploy run that built it: tests and image scans, Terraform, then smoke and load checks against the live site.
